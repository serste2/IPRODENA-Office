From research to launch

The line starts from a simple operational problem: agricultural biomass is often treated as waste or fire risk. Inula delle Grotte turns public plant research and local field work into kits that help redirect biomass, soil care and observation into a regenerative practice.

The kit pathway

Each kit connects one practical action to one research layer.

ATA and #iononbrucio

The public launch connects the product line to a local environmental action: reducing open-fire biomass management, creating a practical collection pathway for healthy olive prunings, and supporting the work of ATA for the Trasubbie and Trasubbino area.
